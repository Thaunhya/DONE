Our Story

Founded as a next-generation hosting and cloud solutions provider, DelaOne was launched to meet the growing demands of developers, digital innovators, and emerging businesses seeking reliable, cost-effective, and high-performance infrastructure.

Backed by Delaplex, a global technology leader established in 2004, DelaOne was created to simplify complex cloud computing and deliver enterprise-grade solutions to startups and SMBs without enterprise-level complexity.

Our journey began with a singular vision: to build a powerful yet accessible platform that enables creators and businesses to launch, scale, and succeed online.
